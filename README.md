# SONOFF Dongle Series Open-Source Firmware

SONOFF sincerely appreciates the third-party developers who create and maintain open-source firmware for the SONOFF Dongle Series. Their contributions give users more choice and continue to strengthen the SONOFF developer community.

This repository enables SONOFF Dongle Flasher to support third-party open-source firmware for SONOFF Dongle Series products. It currently supports firmware from [Nerivec](https://github.com/Nerivec), with plans to include more third-party firmware in the future.

We extend our sincere thanks to [Nerivec](https://github.com/Nerivec), [Koenkk](https://github.com/Koenkk), and every developer who contributes their time and expertise to the open-source community. SONOFF is proud to sponsor Nerivec and Koenkk, and we welcome more developers to create and share open-source firmware for the SONOFF Dongle Series.
